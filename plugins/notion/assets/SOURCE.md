# Notion icon source

The Notion glyph is the current vector published by
[Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/notion.svg),
verified against Notion's official website favicon on 2026-08-29. The glyph path
is unchanged; a neutral white tile was added so the black mark remains legible
in both light and dark GNOME themes.

Notion and its logo are trademarks of Notion Labs, Inc.
