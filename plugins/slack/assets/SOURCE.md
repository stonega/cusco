# Slack icon source

`slack.svg` is Slack's official color mark served by the Slack media site at
<https://a.slack-edge.com/38f0e7c/marketing/img/nav/logo.svg>, retrieved on
2026-08-29. Only the accessible `<title>` element and formatting were added;
the official geometry and colors are unchanged.

Use of the mark is subject to Slack's
[media-kit and brand guidelines](https://slack.com/media-kit).
