# Slack write safety

## Always review before consequential writes

Ask for explicit confirmation immediately before:

- `@channel`, `@here`, or `@everyone` broadcasts;
- posting confidential information outside its source audience;
- messaging a large, unfamiliar, or externally shared channel;
- creating conversations, inviting people, or sending multiple messages;
- bulk reactions or changes across messages; or
- replacing substantial canvas content.

Show the exact destination, audience, message, and important side effects. A
request to draft, summarize, translate, or improve text does not authorize a
post.

## Preserve boundaries

Do not reveal private-channel, DM, restricted-file, or retained historical
content to a wider audience without explicit authorization. If a safe summary
can satisfy the goal, propose it without including sensitive details.

## Partial failures

Stop after a failed or partial write, report what Slack accepted, and ask before
retrying any operation that could duplicate messages, reactions, or content.
